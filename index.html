Privacy & data

Your prompts stay yours

Everything you type, save and teach stays in this browser on this device. Nothing leaves it unless you turn on sharing below.

What gets sent when sharing is on

  • The idea you typed, after copying a prompt (emails and phone numbers are removed first)
  • The mode (General, Code, Art), the structure, and the kind of job the engine read
  • Whether you edited the finished prompt, and your "Did it get your meaning?" answer
  • The app version and the date

Never sent

  • Your name, account, email, location or device details
  • Saved prompts, drafts, or anything you didn't copy
